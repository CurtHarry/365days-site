Privacy Policy

365 Days: Growth · Effective 23 November 2026

This policy explains what information the 365 Days: Growth app ("the app") uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. The app is provided by Curtis Harry ("we", "us"). If you have any question about this policy, write to support@365daysgrowth.com.

In short: the app has no user accounts, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. Your favourites, notes, goals and settings stay on your device. We never see them and never sell any information.

1. Information kept only on your device

The following is stored on your phone or tablet and is not sent to us:

  • sayings you mark as favourites, and your notes;
  • goals, milestones and progress you record;
  • your settings, such as reminder times, theme and widget choices;
  • which sayings you have already seen, so each day shows a new one.

Uninstalling the app deletes this information from your device.

2. Optional backup to your own Google Drive (Android)

If you choose to turn on backup, the app asks you to sign in with Google and uploads a backup file of the information in section 1 to a folder called "365 Days:Growth Backups" in your own Google Drive. You can make backups yourself or schedule them. The app asks only for permission to work with files it creates itself (Google's "drive.file" access); it cannot see your other Drive files. The backup goes straight from your device to Google over an encrypted connection; we never receive it. Google's handling of your Drive is covered by the Google Privacy Policy. You can delete backups in Google Drive at any time, and you can withdraw the app's access in your Google Account settings.

You can also save a backup file to your device instead; that file stays wherever you put it.

3. Subscriptions

Subscriptions are bought through Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your name, card or payment details; the store handles payment under its own privacy policy.

To know whether your subscription or free trial is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives a random identifier created by the app (not your name or email), your purchase and subscription history from the store, and basic technical information such as device type, app version and approximate country (worked out from your IP address). RevenueCat processes this on our behalf only to manage subscriptions and give us totals such as how many people subscribe. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

4. Book details, author photos and links

To show where a book can be found, the app may look up the book's ISBN with Google Books and Open Library, and it may load author photos from Wikipedia. These requests contain only the book number or image address. Like any internet request, they also reveal your device's IP address to that service. The app uses your device's region setting (for example United Kingdom) to choose the right Amazon store; it does not use your location.

If you tap a link to Amazon, a library or another website, that website opens in your browser and its own privacy policy applies. Some links to Amazon may be affiliate links: if you buy, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Amazon does not tell us who you are or what you bought.

5. Sharing, reminders, read-aloud and reviews

  • Sharing: when you share a saying, your device's own share menu sends it only to the app or person you choose.
  • Reminders and the home-screen widget: these are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Read aloud: uses your device's text-to-speech engine. The app sends no text to us.
  • Ratings: the app may occasionally show the store's own rating window. Anything you write goes to Google or Apple, not to us.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not show advertising.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools.
  • We do not sell or rent information, or share it for advertising.
  • We do not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.

8. How long information is kept

Information on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Backups stay in your Google Drive until you delete them. RevenueCat keeps subscription records while your subscription is active and afterwards for as long as needed for accounting and legal reasons, after which they are deleted or made anonymous.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to see, correct, delete or receive a copy of personal information about you, to object to or limit its use, and to complain to a data protection authority: in France, the CNIL (cnil.fr), or the authority where you live, such as the Information Commissioner's Office in the UK (ico.org.uk). Most information never leaves your device, so you control it directly. For subscription records, email support@365daysgrowth.com and we will act within one month. Because the app has no accounts, we may ask for the order number from your Google Play or App Store receipt email to find your record.

10. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Security

All information the app sends over the internet uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update it here and change the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
